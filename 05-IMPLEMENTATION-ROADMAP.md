# Implementation Roadmap

## Strategic Delivery Model
The university should not implement all requirements at once. The most effective path is a phased digital transformation with controlled governance and evidence-based release.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation and Governance
**Duration:** 1–3 months

### Scope
- define vision and stakeholders
- set governance structure
- identify core modules and data owners
- confirm academic and compliance policies
- define the MVP

### Deliverables
- project governance charter
- requirements baseline
- data ownership map
- risk register
- compliance and AI governance policies

---

## Phase 2 — Student & Academic Core
**Duration:** 3–6 months

### Scope
- admissions workflow
- student records
- curriculum and course catalog
- enrollment and registration
- timetable and academic planning
- attendance and academic dashboards

### Deliverables
- student master records
- academic master data
- admissions workflow
- course management
- class scheduling and attendance

---

## Phase 3 — Learning, Assessment, and Advising
**Duration:** 4–8 months

### Scope
- classroom and online learning support
- assignment workflows
- exams and results
- rubrics and assessment mapping
- learning analytics
- advising and student support tracking

### Deliverables
- LMS integration strategy
- assessment framework
- student learning dashboard
- faculty reporting dashboard

---

## Phase 4 — Finance, HR, and Operations
**Duration:** 4–8 months

### Scope
- fee structure and payments
- scholarships and refunds
- payroll and HR workflows
- library and hostel support
- campus operations and service tickets

### Deliverables
- finance and accounting modules
- payroll and leave management
- student support service workflows
- operational dashboards

---

## Phase 5 — Compliance, Security, and Audit Readiness
**Duration:** 2–4 months

### Scope
- RBAC and identity management
- audit trails
- backup and disaster recovery
- cybersecurity monitoring
- compliance and HEC reporting
- AI governance and content review

### Deliverables
- security controls
- access review process
- audit evidence and reporting
- incident response procedures

---

## Phase 6 — Pilot and Scale Testing
**Duration:** 2–4 months

### Scope
- pilot selected departments/programs
- measure service quality and adoption
- resolve issues from real users
- validate academic quality and impact

### Deliverables
- pilot findings report
- issues log and fixes
- adoption and satisfaction metrics
- rollout readiness decision

---

## Phase 7 — Campus-Wide Expansion
**Duration:** 3–6 months

### Scope
- expand by campus or department
- standardize SOPs
- reinforce support model
- train users and leaders

### Deliverables
- phased rollout plan
- support process setup
- KPI tracking dashboard
- operational go-live execution

---

## Phase 8 — Optimization, AI Expansion, and Continuous Improvement
**Duration:** ongoing

### Scope
- predictive analytics
- personalized AI advising
- workforce improvement and benchmarking
- continuous platform enhancement
- institutional scale-up planning

### Deliverables
- improvement backlog
- AI governance reviews
- benchmark reporting
- product roadmap for future scale

---

## Suggested Executive Decision Gates
1. Requirements signoff  
2. MVP approval  
3. Pilot readiness  
4. Phase 3 go-live approval  
5. Full rollout approval  

---

## Final Message
Successful university digital transformation depends on controlled rollout, governance, and evidence-based improvement—not just feature delivery.
