# Roles and Responsibilities

## Governance Structure
A successful university digital transformation needs clear ownership across academic, administrative, technical, and compliance domains.

## Executive Steering Committee
**Role:** strategic oversight  
**Members:** Rector, Deans, Registrar, Finance lead, HR lead, IT lead, quality assurance lead

### Responsibilities
- approve roadmap and funding
- resolve strategic conflicts
- review KPIs and major risks
- approve major releases and scale decisions

---

## Academic Workstream
**Owner:** Academic leadership / Deans / HODs

### Responsibilities
- define academic rules and learning outcomes
- validate curriculum structures and assessment frameworks
- review AI-assisted teaching and assessment workflows
- sign off on academic changes

---

## Student Services Workstream
**Owner:** Registrar / Student Affairs lead

### Responsibilities
- admissions process definition
- registration and enrollment policies
- student issue and grievance handling
- graduation and transcript workflows

---

## Finance and HR Workstream
**Owner:** Finance Lead / HR Lead

### Responsibilities
- fee structures and receivables
- payroll and leave workflows
- scholarships and refunds
- financial reporting and controls

---

## IT and Security Workstream
**Owner:** CIO / IT Director / Security Lead

### Responsibilities
- system architecture and integrations
- identity and access control
- cybersecurity and backups
- monitoring, support, and incident response

---

## Data and Compliance Workstream
**Owner:** Quality Assurance / Compliance Manager

### Responsibilities
- audit trails and retention policies
- HEC and regulatory reporting
- quality assurance and accreditation tracking
- data governance and master data standards

---

## Product / Delivery Workstream
**Owner:** Project Manager / Product Lead

### Responsibilities
- coordinate requirements and backlog
- manage release planning
- organize testing and pilot cycles
- provide communication and training support

---

## Key Governance Principle
No critical user role or process should be approved without explicit business owner accountability.
