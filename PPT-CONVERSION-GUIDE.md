# PowerPoint Conversion Guide

## Goal
Turn the written requirements and analysis into a visual executive presentation in Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides, or equivalent format.

## Recommended Slide Deck Structure
Keep the deck between 25 and 40 slides for executive clarity.

### Deck Outline
1. Title
2. Executive summary
3. Business problem
4. Vision and strategic goals
5. Stakeholders and users
6. Student journey
7. Functional areas overview
8. Academic foundations
9. Teaching and assessment
10. Admissions and student services
11. Finance, HR, and campus services
12. Security and compliance
13. Management dashboards and KPIs
14. Technology architecture
15. Integration approach
16. Risks and mitigation
17. Delivery roadmap
18. Phased implementation plan
19. Benefits by stakeholder
20. Recommendation and next steps

---

## Suggested PowerPoint Design Rules
- Use one key message per slide
- Keep text short: headlines + 3–5 bullet points
- Use icons and color coding by service area
- Add architecture diagrams and process flows
- Use a consistent theme across all slides
- Limit each slide to one core visual focal point

---

## Best Practice for Diagram Insertion
Use Mermaid diagrams, architecture screenshots, or simple flowchart visuals. Convert to PNG or SVG where needed for final presentation.

---

## Suggested Visuals
- student journey flow
- university architecture diagram
- module map
- KPI dashboard mockup
- resource and governance framework
- implementation roadmap timeline

---

## Final Presentation Tips
- Keep the story simple and leadership-focused
- Connect every requirement to an operational or business outcome
- Keep technical details in backup sections or appendix slides
- Make compliance and governance visible in the story arc
- Use a pilot-first message to reduce perceived risk

---

## Recommended Delivery Format
- Executive deck: 25–35 slides
- Appendix: detailed 100-point requirement matrix
- Separate one-page board brief
