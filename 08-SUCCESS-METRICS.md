# Success Metrics and KPIs

## Core KPI Categories
The university should monitor whether the system is delivering real operational and academic value.

### 1. Student Experience KPIs
- application-to-enrollment conversion rate
- registration completion time
- average support response time
- student satisfaction score
- issue closure time

### 2. Academic Performance KPIs
- course completion rate
- assessment pass rate
- attendance rate
- early-warning intervention effectiveness
- graduation rate by program

### 3. Operational Efficiency KPIs
- manual processing time per student case
- fee collection turnaround time
- number of unresolved records or exceptions
- average time to approve academic changes
- resource utilization rate

### 4. Financial KPIs
- fee collection rate
- outstanding receivables balance
- scholarship processing time
- budget variance and reporting timeliness

### 5. Security and Compliance KPIs
- audit log completeness
- policy exceptions detected and resolved
- incident response time
- backup restore test success
- compliance reporting accuracy and timeliness

### 6. AI Governance KPIs
- review coverage for AI-assisted academic content
- number of AI issues reported and resolved
- transparency rate for AI-use disclosures
- accuracy and correctness of AI-generated educational support

---

## Measurement Cadence
- daily: system uptime and support efficiency
- weekly: operational throughput and support queue health
- monthly: adoption, KPl trends, and exception counts
- quarterly: student outcomes and leadership review

---

## Important Principle
Metrics should reflect actual student and operational outcomes—not just software adoption.
